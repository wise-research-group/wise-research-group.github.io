Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site, including people who use screen readers, keyboards, magnification, or reduced-motion settings.

Our goal

This site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. LSU measures its web pages against the same standard, and it is the standard in the U.S. Department of Justice’s ADA Title II rule for the web content of state and local governments.

What we do

  • Pages have clear headings, landmarks, and a “Skip to content” link, and are built to work with a keyboard alone.
  • Photos and diagrams have text alternatives. Purely decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Videos are short, silent loops of tests and simulations, each with a text description. They play only while they are on screen.
  • When a device is set to reduce motion, the animated wind on the home page and the looping videos stay still, and the home page slideshow does not advance on its own. The slideshow can also be paused at any time.
  • Pages are checked at phone and desktop widths with automated accessibility tests (axe-core), including color contrast. The most recent check was in November 2026.

Known limitations

  • The PDF documents on this site, the short CV and the facility fact sheet, are not yet tagged for screen readers. Accessible versions are available on request.
  • Links to journals, Google Scholar, and other websites lead to pages we do not control.
  • Automated tests cannot find every barrier, so reports from visitors help.

Report a problem

If anything on this site is hard to use, email aly@lsu.edu with the page address and a short description. We will reply, fix the problem where we can, and provide the information in another format if you need it. You can also use LSU’s accessibility reporting page.
